About

01

I work at the intersection of machine learning and engineering — integrating physical principles and domain knowledge to support reliable analysis, interpretable predictions, and informed design decisions.

I am particularly interested in self-supervised learning of 3D geometric representations for maritime perception that is robust to partial observations, viewpoint changes, and limited real-world data. I also seek to explore simulation-informed learning and multi-fidelity modeling for structural analysis and optimization, balancing computational efficiency with physical understanding and engineering constraints.
